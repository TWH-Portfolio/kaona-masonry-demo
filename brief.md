## Anti-Patterns Avoided

- No gradient-icon-containers
- No soft-radii (--radius: 0rem throughout)
- No gradient-accent-text on headlines
- No more than 2 §-kicker sections per page
- No icon-only-layout (8 real Unsplash photos across pages)
- No abstract-trust-tagline ("Built on Trust", etc.)
- No generic-values-h2 ("Our Commitment")
- No quality-craftsmanship copy
- No em-dash-overuse in user-visible copy
- No round-number-stats (483, 17, 31 are specific)
- No Google Maps iframe with `?key=YOUR_KEY` — using keyless embed format
